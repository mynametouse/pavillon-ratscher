### What to build (in this order; first usable increment within ~2 h)
1. **Pure logic with unit tests** (no three.js, no DOM – Phase 2 rule "Logik ohne Browser"; `viz3/test/unit/gplan_*.test.mjs`, run by `node --test` via tools/check.sh):
   - Growth: size at an age (planting height `pflanzhoehe_m` → final `hoehe_m`/`breite_m`, saturating over `jahre_bis_endgroesse`, deterministic, mid of the ranges unless a seed varies it slightly), season by month (leaves: bare / budding / full / autumn colour by `laub`; bloom by `bluete.monate`; fruit by `ernte.monate`).
   - Shade: sun position for a date and time (use the same astronomy as `time/daynight.js` DN – import a pure function or reimplement the standard formula for 50.4991° N, 10.7692° E, CET/CEST), a plant's shadow footprint on flat ground (crown ellipsoid + trunk projected along the sun), and the share of a day a point lies in shade of the planned plants (sample hours) – for "needs sun but stands in shade".
   - Rules / adviser (German texts with the reason; use `regeln.json` `fakten` where they fit): boundary distance (ThürNRG category of the plant → distance from the trunk centre to the property boundary), distance of the grown crown to buildings (pavilion, bungalow, Holzhaus, greenhouse, carport, wash house, workshop) and to paths/the bridge, wet ground near the brook in the north (plants with `staunaesse: 'nein'` or `feuchte` without 'feucht'/'nass' near it), late frost in the valley (early bloom with `spaetfrost: 'hoch'`), incompatible neighbours (`unvertraeglich`, '*' with `radius_m` or the crown), missing pollinator (`befruchtung.selbstfruchtbar: 'nein'` and no other cultivar of the same species within ~30 m in the plan; say which would fit from `befruchter`), crowns overlapping when grown, light (needs 'sonne' but shaded more than about half of a June day), voles/goats → protection at planting (`schutz`). Each warning: level (Hinweis / Warnung), plant(s), short text, the reason.
