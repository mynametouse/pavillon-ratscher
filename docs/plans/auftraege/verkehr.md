## Your topic: life on the streets – people strolling, cars driving, stopping cars
Falko (25.10.2026), in his words: „Außerdem sollen ab und zu auch Leute einfach durchs Dorf schlendern oder auch mal mit dem Auto fahren. Auch in Schleusingen. Ich möchte die Autos auch anhalten können. Wenn es sich anbietet. Ähnlich wie bei GTA.“ – and: everyone in the village says du to the player.
Goal, in this order (first usable increment within ~2–3 h, then the rest):
1. **Pedestrians in Ratscher**: now and then a person walks along the village streets and paths (on the pavement/edge of the road, not through houses or fences), at a natural pace, sometimes with a dog or a bag, stops briefly (looks at a front garden, greets the player in passing with a short line as a subtitle – du!), and walks on until out of sight; a handful at a time at most, by daytime more, at night almost none (game clock `time/clock.js` CLOCK). Reuse the rigged Rocketbox figures and their clips (`npc/npc_real.js` rbPerson/prepModel, `dorf/dorf_people.js` – loaded only when near, freed when far; see how the villagers are loaded and dropped). The eight named villagers (`dorf/dorf_text.js`, `dorf/dorf_life.js`) keep their own everyday – your strollers are other people (give each a German first name, age, gender and a one-line role, e.g. „Rentnerin mit Dackel“, so the free conversations can use them, see 5). If you need more figure variety, look at how the figures are built (`tools/player/`, `tools/chars/`, `viz3/extra/dorf/manifest.json`, CHARMAN) – new figure files go to `viz3/extra/verkehr/dist/**` (each file < 5 MB, open licences only, list the source in the extra's `credits_add`); vary clothing colours/hair where the model allows. Never more than the memory budget allows on an iPhone: few figures loaded at once, shared materials, dropped when far (measure: `viz3/test/memphone.py`, `viz3/test/memprof.py`).
2. **Cars in Ratscher**: now and then a car drives through the village on the real streets (`world/roads.js` ROADS: the ways, lanes, right-hand traffic), in at one edge and out at another, at village speed (30–50 km/h), braking for curves and junctions, wheels turning, headlights at night. The villagers' own cars (`dorf/dorf_cars.js` DCARS) already drive out and back – reuse its car model and driving code where it fits (extend DCARS minimally or build a shared module), do not break them.
3. **Schleusingen**: the same there – people on the market square and streets, a few cars on the streets of the town (`welt/schleusingen.js`, its streets/tiles, `welt/welt.js` WELT, `KERN.zone`: build when near, drop when far). And on the drive between the places (`welt/fahrt.js`) an oncoming car now and then if cheap.
4. **Stopping cars (Falko: „ähnlich wie bei GTA“, but friendly)**: cars brake for the player and for pedestrians in their lane (never drive through the player; honk if he stays), and the player can **wave a car down** when it comes towards him (action button „Auto anhalten“ when a car approaches within ~40 m in his direction; the driver brakes and stops beside him). Then, at the stopped car: „Mit dem Fahrer reden“ (see 5), „Mitfahren“ (get in as a passenger – the car drives on to its destination or the next village place, then lets him out; a short ride is enough) and „Selbst fahren“ (the driver gets out, grumbles or agrees, and the player drives that car with the existing vehicle controls – `vehicles/vehicle.js`, `vehicles/car.js` (the player's own car, Logan MCV size) – it must handle like the player's car; when he leaves it, the driver gets back in some time later or it is fetched; nothing is stolen for good, no violence, no damage system). If something here does not fit the code well, choose the simpler friendly variant and say so in your report.
5. **Talking to strangers and drivers**: the Kern-Chat is adding free voice conversations with any person (`npc/ki.js`, today with Hubert and Ronald, `docs/features/ki.md`); it will offer `KI.person({ key, name, gender: 'm' | 'f', age, role, sheet, pos: () => [x, y], fig, panel })` and `KI.talk(key)` on `src` (merge `origin/src` when it lands; check `git log origin/src -- viz3/src/npc/ki.js`). Until then give each person a short German greeting and two or three small-talk lines (du!) shown in a small talk panel in the look of the neighbours' (`#npcpanel`, `shell/css/stage3d.css`; the villagers' `#dorfpanel` in `dorf/dorf.js` is a good model). Keep the person data (name, gender, age, role, a two-sentence character sheet in German) in one plain data module so the Kern-Chat can register them with KI.
- Use the core services (`viz3/src/core/kern.js`, docs/ARCHITECTURE.md „Dienste für Erweiterungen“): `KERN.tick` (never window.TICKS), `KERN.act` for the action button, `KERN.zone` for Schleusingen, `KERN.place`, `KERN.on/emit` (emit e.g. 'verkehr:angehalten' { car }, 'verkehr:mitfahrt', 'verkehr:selbst'), saving only through `SAVE` (`core/save.js`) if you need to save anything (probably not). Lights: never create a new point light (it recompiles every shader) – headlights as emissive meshes/sprites or `KERN.lights.borrow`. Performance: instancing or merged meshes, few draw calls, nothing heavy per frame for things far away; the frame rate on a laptop must not drop noticeably (`viz3/test/prof.py`, `KERN.profile(true)`).
