## Your topic: buildings in Suhl the player can enter – especially in the Zentrum, the Suhler Aue and Suhl-Nord

Pick about six, two per area, by what is typical and what the photos/descriptions allow, e.g.:
- **Zentrum**: the Hauptkirche St. Marien (nave, altar, organ, pews – research its real interior: Baroque?), the Waffenmuseum in the Malzhaus (exhibition rooms with display cases of hunting guns and the history of the gunsmiths – generic, no real weapon models in detail), or the foyer and big hall of the Congress Centrum Suhl (CCS); a café or bakery on the Steinweg/Markt.
- **Suhler Aue**: the club house of the sports field (changing rooms, club room with trophies and a bar, the view onto the pitch), a stairwell and a flat in a residential block of the Aue.
- **Suhl-Nord**: a WBS 70 Plattenbau block – entrance with letter boxes, the stairwell (no lift in a 5-storey block; lift in 11-storey ones), one furnished flat (typical DDR plan: kitchen with window to the stairwell side or a „Durchreiche“, bathroom cell, living room with balcony) – and a supermarket or the Kaufhalle/shop centre of Suhl-Nord, or a school.
Each interior: a door (the action button „Eintreten“/„Hinausgehen“ via `KERN.act`), walls/floors/ceiling that stop and carry the player, light by day and night (borrowed lights only: `KERN.lights.borrow`, never new point lights), furniture you can sit on where it fits, a place name (`KERN.place`), the events 'suhl:enter'/'suhl:leave'. Built only when the player is at the door or inside (lazy, sliced over frames), freed when he leaves the building's surroundings; no new shader programs. Register each with `SUHL.detail(osmId, { build, free })` so the generic block is replaced by your building with matching outside walls and a real door; until SUHL exists, test against a stand-in. Export `SUHLIN.rooms` = [{ id, name, area: 'zentrum'|'aue'|'nord', door: [x, y], spots: { … } }] so `suhl-leute` can place people inside (e.g. the sexton in the church, the club treasurer in the club house, a pensioner in the Plattenbau flat).
